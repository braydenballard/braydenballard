Top story: West Allen water customers, read this

Three updates from the West Allen Parish Water District this month:

  • Pay to avoid a shutoff. Lockouts for unpaid bills (due Oct. 15) were scheduled for Monday, Oct. 26. If your water's off, call the office to settle up.
  • Paperless billing is here. The district now offers e-Bill, so you can get your bill by email instead of mail.
  • Boil advisory lifted. The Hwy 113 advisory north of Bel Fire Tower Road, issued Oct. 18 after repairs, was lifted Oct. 23.

The district's next board meeting is Tuesday, Nov. 10 at 6 p.m. Questions about your bill? Call the office at 337-749-2589 (Monday–Thursday 8 a.m.–4 p.m., Friday 8 a.m.–noon).

What your leaders decided

  • A new parish economic development district is now law: HB 247 became Act 398 this year. It took effect without the Governor's signature. It creates the Allen Parish Economic Development District, which covers the whole parish, and abolishes the Allen Parish Tourist Commission. A five-member board runs it, with seats tied to the Allen Parish Industrial Development Board, the Oakdale and Kinder chambers of commerce, and the Police Jury. The district is allowed to charge a hotel and campground tax of up to 3%. (La. Legislature, Act 398)
  • Oakdale City Council: The Oct. 1 agenda included an ordinance creating a city recreation committee, a request to advertise a public hearing on an ordinance about boring in the city (up for adoption in November), giving up an unused, never-built section of Emily Street, and police hires: two part-time officers and a part-time dispatcher. Next regular meeting: Thursday, Nov. 5, 6:30 p.m., City Hall courtroom, 333 E. 6th Ave. (City of Oakdale Facebook page, Oct. 1 agenda)
  • Police Jury: Next meeting is Monday, Nov. 2, 6 p.m., 602 Court St., Oberlin. (allenparishpolicejury.com)
  • Coming up: West Allen Parish Water District board meets Tuesday, Nov. 10, 6 p.m.
